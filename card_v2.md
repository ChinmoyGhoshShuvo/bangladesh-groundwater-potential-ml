**Title:** Groundwater Potential Mapping of Bangladesh (Random Forest vs ANN)

**Summary:** I mapped national groundwater potential from 19 topographic, climatic, socio-economic and geological factors, using 2,144 sample points to train Random Forest and neural-network models. Random Forest was the more accurate model (AUC 0.97 vs 0.91). Its map shows 35.73% of the country with high or very high potential, while the hill districts have the lowest potential.
